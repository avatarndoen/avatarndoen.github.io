Mechanism Development

Asymmetric rolling profiles tune directional motion. Two stacked modules provide flexion–extension; an orthogonal module adds radial–ulnar deviation.

Parametric Design

Parameterized wrist size, rolling profiles, and flexure dimensions through 21 geometric variables in a SolidWorks master sketch.

Automated CAD–CAE

Linked SolidWorks API and ANSYS ACT to automate geometry, contacts, boundary conditions, meshing, solving, and response extraction across four motion directions.

CAD Automation

Experimental Setup & Motion Validation

Built a motor-driven tendon-pulling rig with inline force measurement to test the printed wrist’s range of motion. Measured motion differed only slightly from the surrogate predictions in all tested directions.

Scalable Designs

Generated five optimized wrist sizes for prescribed motion and stiffness targets, then re-evaluated each geometry using FEA.

Surrogate-Assisted Multi-Objective Optimization

Trained machine-learning surrogates on FEA data and combined them with U-NSGA-III to optimize range of motion and initial/final stiffness under stress constraints.

Custom BLDC–Cycloidal Actuators

Actuator Architecture

Integrated a frameless BLDC motor, cycloidal reducer, shafts, bearings, and magnetic encoder within a custom housing. Thicker structural sections compensate for the limited stiffness of 3D-printed components.

Component color key: Grey: Housing & Structure · Green: Bearings · Cyan: Rotor · Red: Stator · Purple: Output Assembly · Pink: Cycloidal Discs 1 & 2 · Brown: Eccentric Shaft

Cycloidal Transmission

Designed a 12:1 cycloidal reducer housed inside the motor rotor to balance torque amplification, compact packaging, and backdrivability.

Motor Control & Precision Testing

Demonstrated high-speed rotation with minimal visible runout. A preliminary dial-indicator return test measured approximately 0.05 mm deviation at a 200 mm radius.

BLDC Motor Runout

Wearable Master Controller

Wearable Master Mechanism

Designed a wearable linkage for shoulder, elbow, forearm, and wrist motion, with an open-center, two-DoF wrist allowing hand passage.

Encoder & IMU Sensing

Four absolute magnetic encoders capture elbow, forearm, and wrist motion; one IMU estimates upper-arm orientation for shoulder tracking.

Seven-DoF Slave Robotic Arm

Seven-DoF Slave Arm Architecture

Built a seven-DoF arm with five custom BLDC–cycloidal actuators and two wrist servos, using 3D-printed structures reinforced with carbon-fiber tubes.

System Integration & Motion Testing

Integrated the wearable master and slave arm for real-time teleoperation, gravity compensation, and motion recording/playback.

Gravity Compensation

Design Failure & Mechanical Iteration

Inspection traced elastic angular deflection under load to output-pin bending despite negligible free play, prompting an upgrade to larger-diameter pins.

Next Integration Step

Planned: integrate the tendon-driven hand for teleoperated pick-and-place testing.

Mechanical Design & Tendon Architecture

Built an underactuated continuum robot with alternating compliant joints, six pulling tendons, and bending in two perpendicular planes. Tendon routing lets each actuator bend its section while the others largely retain their bending angles.

Gripper

Rolling-Contact Modeling & Inverse Kinematics

Developed a dynamic model to evaluate the design and used inverse kinematics to calculate tendon displacements for open-loop actuator commands.
