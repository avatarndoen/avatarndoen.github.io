I developed a kinematic model to study how linkage dimensions and noncircular gear profiles affected speed and torque ripple. I then taught myself surrogate-assisted optimization to search for geometries with lower torque ripple. This reduced the model’s mean RMS torque ripple across the tested ratio settings. I later applied what I learned to my prosthetic-wrist research.
