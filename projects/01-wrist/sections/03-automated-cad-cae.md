Comparing many designs manually would have been impractical, so I connected automated CAD generation with finite-element analysis. I used geometry and face metadata to automate boundary conditions, contacts, meshing, solving, and extraction of motion, stiffness, and stress responses. This produced the simulation data I needed to compare designs and train surrogate models.
