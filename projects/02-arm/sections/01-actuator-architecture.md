To provide sufficient torque while keeping the joints backdrivable, I designed quasi-direct-drive actuators around a frameless BLDC outrunner motor and a cycloidal transmission. I integrated the motor, shafts, bearings, encoder, and output within a custom housing.

Thicker structural sections compensate for the limited stiffness of the printed components.

**Component color key:** Grey: Housing & Structure · Green: Bearings · Cyan: Rotor · Red: Stator · Purple: Output Assembly · Pink: Cycloidal Discs 1 & 2 · Brown: Eccentric Shaft
