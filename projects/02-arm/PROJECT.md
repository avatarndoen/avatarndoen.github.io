I built this arm to gain more hands-on experience with robotic hardware and control. I designed custom quasi-direct-drive actuators around the torque and backdrivability I wanted, then built a wearable master controller so I could move the robot through my own arm movements. I particularly enjoyed bringing the mechanisms, sensing, and control together into a complete system.
