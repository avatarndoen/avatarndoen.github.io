ABOUT / BACKGROUND

A little more about me.

Mechanical engineer. Curious maker. Always learning.

I'm Avatar, a mechanical engineering graduate from Institut Teknologi Bandung. I enjoy designing mechanisms, building robots, and using computational methods to improve their design.

For my undergraduate thesis, I designed a compliant prosthetic wrist and developed a CAD–CAE automation and machine-learning-assisted optimization workflow to personalize its motion and stiffness. My independent projects include compact actuators, a teleoperated robotic arm and wearable controller, a tendon-driven hand, and a continuum robot.

I currently work on automotive sensor integration in Toyota's Body Electronics Design Division in Japan. Outside work, I continue to design, build, and test my own robotics projects.

What draws me to research

My undergraduate thesis introduced me to biomechanics and machine learning. Working through unfamiliar design, automation, and optimization problems made me enjoy the process of research: asking questions, learning by doing, and testing ideas in hardware.

Where I want to go

I want to develop robotic devices for healthcare, including assistive systems that support natural movement and surgical manipulators for precise tasks. My projects have given me a starting point in mechanisms, actuation, computational design, and teleoperation. I want to build on that foundation through further research.

Beyond engineering

Outside engineering, I enjoy basketball, gaming, and anime.
